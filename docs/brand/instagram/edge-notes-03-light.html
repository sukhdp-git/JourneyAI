Why it keeps happening,
and the fix.

  1. 1Your stop is where everyone's stop is.Obvious highs and lows get swept. Put it beyond the structure, plus the spread and a small buffer.
  2. 2A tight stop isn't lower risk.Risk = lot size × stop distance. Wider stop, fewer lots, same dollars at risk:
0.20 lots × $5 stopXAUUSD · $100 risk
=
0.10 lots × $10 stopXAUUSD · $100 risk
  1. 3Count it in your journal.Tag every "stopped out, then hit TP". If it keeps happening, your stops are too tight, not your analysis.
