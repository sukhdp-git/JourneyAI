3 questions to answer after every trade.

  1. 1Why did I enter?Setup, level, reason. One sentence.
  2. 2How did I feel?Calm, bored, anxious, revenge. One word is enough.
  3. 3Did I follow my plan?Yes or no. Be honest. Nobody else is reading.
Journaling doesn't make trading easy. It makes it understandable.
