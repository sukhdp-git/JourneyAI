Before you post (every time)

  1. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps. First frame already moving; no logo intro.
  2. Captions: bold white with a black outline, max 2 words per line, in the middle of the screen. Keep them out of the bottom ~20% and the right edge, where Instagram's buttons and caption sit.
  3. Same signature every reel: the violet→pink flash + shutter sound at 0s, the small journzey watermark top-left, key words in the brand gradient.
  4. Your real voice. Use AI voice only for animated reels.
  5. Real numbers only. Anything made up gets a small "example" label.
  6. Exactly ONE call to action. Rotate comment / save / send across reels.
  7. Last frame = first frame. No fade to black, no end card.
  8. Original audio or trending audio at low volume under your voice.
  9. Upload a custom cover (big 3-word title) so the grid looks like a series.
  10. Reply to every comment in the first hour. Test two hooks with Trial Reels.
