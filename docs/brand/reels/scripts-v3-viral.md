# journzey: 8 viral reel scripts (v3)

Built for the 9-step structure: hook, pattern interrupt, curiosity loop, visual story, retention, emotion, payoff, share trigger, repeatable format.
All scripts are 8–14 seconds. Demo data shown on screen must carry a small "Demo account" label.
End card on every reel: **journzey is coming soon · Follow for launch day 🔔**

## Why the last reels lost people at second 3

1. **The hook started too late.** Frame 1 must already be moving and already have text on it. There should be no logo, no "hey guys", and no slow zoom.
2. **The hook explained instead of opening a question.** The first line should make a viewer think "wait, what?" instead of "okay, I get it".
3. **The first cut came after 2 seconds or more.** Cut, or change something on screen, every 0.8–1.5 seconds.
4. **The payoff was obvious.** If people can guess the ending, they leave. Withhold the answer until the last 2 seconds.

## Hook rules (first 1 second)

- On-screen text is 6 words or fewer, big, in the centre of the top third.
- Something moves in frame 1: a thumb, a number, or a hard cut.
- Voice and text say the same thing. Many people watch muted.
- Use one of these hook types: **contradiction, direct accusation, a question they can answer, a forbidden instruction, or a POV**.

## 1. "Don't send this to your trading friend."

**Format:** reverse psychology plus a share trigger · **11 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Face to camera, deadpan. Big text. | **"Don't send this to your trading friend."** |
| 1–3 s | Hard cut: phone screen, 3 red trades inside 9 minutes. | "He'll know it's about him." |
| 3–6 s | Lot-size field: 0.50 → 1.00 → 2.00 (each jump on a beat). | "Down two. 'I'll just make it back.' Size doubles." |
| 6–9 s | journzey Mind tab: revenge pattern flagged. *(Demo account)* | "Every single time." |
| 9–11 s | Face again, tiny smile. End card. | "…okay. Send it." |

**Why it spreads:** everyone knows that friend. The "don't" makes people send it.

## 2. "You were about to skip this."

**Format:** meta pattern interrupt · **12 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | A thumb mid-swipe on this reel. Freeze frame. | **"You were about to skip this."** |
| 1–3 s | Slow zoom on the thumb. | "That itch? It's the same one that clicks Buy." |
| 3–7 s | Match cuts: thumb swipes → thumb taps Buy → red number. Repeat 3× and get faster. | (no voice, just 3 sharp sounds) |
| 7–10 s | journzey beep and red banner: daily loss limit hit. | "Impulse doesn't care which app you're in." |
| 10–12 s | Black screen, white text. End card. | **"You didn't skip. That's discipline."** |

**Why it works:** it calls out the exact behaviour happening right now. The ending rewards the viewer for staying.

## 3. "Guess which trade lost the most."

**Format:** a game plus comments · **13 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | 3 trade cards slam in: A, B, C. | **"Guess which one lost the most."** |
| 1–3 s | Cards show entry, lots and time. The emotion tag is blurred. | "Comment A, B or C. Now." |
| 3–7 s | Slow drumroll. A flips (small loss). B flips (win). | "Not A… not B…" |
| 7–10 s | C flips: the biggest loss. Emotion tag unblurs: **"bored"**. | "The one you took because nothing was happening." |
| 10–13 s | journzey emotion breakdown. *(Demo account)* End card. | "Your journal knows your most expensive feeling." |

**Why it works:** people stay to check their guess, and they comment before the reveal, which boosts reach.

## 4. "POV: your trading account can text you."

**Format:** iMessage chat (it's read in real time, so very high retention) · **13 s**

Messages pop in one by one, with a phone vibration sound. The contact is **"My Account 💀"**.

| Time | Message |
|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Text on top: **"POV: your trading account can text you"** · "hey" |
| 1–2 s | "you up?" |
| 2–3 s | "please don't open the chart" |
| 3–4 s | *(you are typing… stops)* |
| 4–5 s | "you opened the chart" |
| 5–6 s | "…" |
| 6–8 s | "it's ok. I'm not mad." |
| 8–10 s | "I just have 23 days left." |
| 10–13 s | Cut to the journzey **Death Clock** ticking. *(Demo account)* End card. |

**Why it spreads:** it's funny for 8 seconds, then it lands like a punch. People send it with "💀 me".

## 5. "Every blown account has one moment."

**Format:** reverse playback (visual story) · **12 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Balance: **$0.00**. Text. | **"Every blown account has one moment."** |
| 1–6 s | VHS rewind effect: the balance climbs back up, trades un-close, red turns into green. Speed ramps. | "Let's rewind." |
| 6–8 s | Hard stop. Freeze on one field: **Lots 0.5 → 2.0**. A red circle draws itself around it. | **"Here."** |
| 8–10 s | Silence for 1 second. | "Not the market. This click." |
| 10–12 s | journzey trade log highlighting the oversized trade. End card. | "Find yours before it finds you." |

**Why it works:** the rewind makes people want to know where it stops.

## 6. "Things traders will never say out loud."

**Format:** a list that turns emotional · **14 s**

Black screen. Each line appears in typewriter style for about 1.5 s, with a soft typing sound and a slow piano note.

1. **"Things traders will never say out loud."**
2. "I lied to my friend about my P&L."
3. "I deposited again. Third time this month."
4. "I've never used a stop loss."
5. "I trade so I don't have to think about my life."
6. *(beat, empty screen)*
7. journzey **Confessions** feed scrolls: anonymous handles, likes counted as "$15 respect". *(Demo account)*
8. "Now there's a place to say it. Anonymously."
9. End card.

**Why it spreads:** line 5 hits hard. Viewers save it and send it privately ("this is you").
*Note: the lines are relatable writing, not quotes from real users. Don't present them as testimonials.*

## 7. "Rate this trader, 1 to 10."

**Format:** expectation flip · **10 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Big green **+$2,000 today**. | **"Rate this trader. 1 to 10."** |
| 1–3 s | Fake comment bubbles pop up: "10", "10 🔥", "teach me". | — |
| 3–6 s | Zoom out: risk shown = **80% of the account on one trade**. | "He risked 80% of his account for it." |
| 6–8 s | The bubbles all flip to "1". | "He's a 1. He just doesn't know yet." |
| 8–10 s | journzey risk-per-trade view. End card. | "Your journal rates the process, not the P&L." |

**Why it works:** the reversal makes people argue in the comments.

## 8. "This video never ends." (loop)

**Format:** seamless loop (replays count as watch time) · **9 s**

| Time | Screen | Voice / text (fast, rhythmic) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Text on screen. | **"Lose."** |
| 1–2 s | Red candle. | "Feel it." |
| 2–3 s | Hand on mouse. | "'Just one more.'" |
| 3–4 s | Red candle, bigger. | "Lose." |
| 4–5 s | Faster. | "Feel it. One more." |
| 5–7 s | Faster again. The screen shakes. | "Lose. Feel it. One more. Lose—" |
| 7–9 s | Hand slams down a phone showing the journzey Log Trade screen. Silence. Text: **"Log it. Break it."** The end card fades straight back into "Lose." | — |

**Why it works:** the end flows into the start, so people watch 2–3 times without noticing. The pattern is what revenge trading feels like.

## Formats to recreate again and again

Keep the structure and swap the topic:

| Format | Structure | Next versions |
|---|---|---|
| **Don't send this** | forbidden instruction → callout → payoff → "okay, send it" | "Don't show this to your wife", "Don't watch this before market open" |
| **Guess which** | 3 choices → comment → reveal → lesson | "Guess which session lost the most", "Guess which emotion" |
| **POV chat** | object texts you → funny → punch | your stop loss texts you, your strategy texts you |
| **Rewind** | end state → rewind → freeze on the moment | the day before a prop firm fail, the trade before a streak broke |
| **Never say out loud** | dark list → hardest line → safe place | "Things your trading group never posts" |
| **Rate this** | show the result → crowd reacts → reveal the hidden cost | rate this win rate, rate this strategy |
| **Loop** | rhythmic repetition → break | "Buy. Hope. Pray. Close." |

**Posting tip:** post one format, see which one keeps people past second 3 in Insights, then make 3 more in that format.
