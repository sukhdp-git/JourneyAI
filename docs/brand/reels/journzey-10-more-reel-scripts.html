that make traders stop and listen.

Emotion first, about 30 seconds each. A mix of trading knowledge, motivation and why journaling matters, written to feel like a conversation, not an advert.

Why the first reel lost people in 2–3 seconds

  • It started with you ("how I built it"), not with them. A stranger doesn't care about your app yet. They care about their own pain. Every script here opens on the viewer's feeling: "You lost five in a row…", "It's 2am…".
  • Nothing happened in the first second. A calm desk shot with a title looks like an intro, and intros get skipped. Start mid-sentence, on a face or on movement, with big text.
  • The hook gave no tension. The best hooks open a loop the viewer needs closed: a shocking number, a confession, or a question about them.
  • Format: landscape footage with black bars and quiet audio. Film vertical and keep the voice loud and close (see the first script file for the full filming guide).

How to deliver the voiceover so people keep listening

  • Talk to ONE person. Say "you", never "guys" or "everyone". Imagine one friend who just blew their account.
  • The hook is slower than the rest. Say the first line slowly and clearly, with a tiny pause before the key word ("…and you open the chart.").
  • Pause before every re-hook (half a second of silence). Silence makes people look up.
  • Lean on the words in CAPITALS in the scripts. Change your pitch on them.
  • Emotional lines: quieter and lower. Knowledge lines: confident and a bit faster.
  • Short sentences only. If a sentence needs a breath in the middle, cut it in two.
  • Record the hook 3–5 times and keep the most natural one. Don't read: look at the line, look away, say it.
  • Mix: post these between the feature reels (first file) so the account is about traders first and the app second.
