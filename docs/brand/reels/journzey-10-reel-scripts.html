How to use this file: film the shots in the "What to record" column, record the voiceover line by line, add the on-screen text as big captions, and post with the caption at the bottom of each script.

How Instagram decides what to push

  • Watch time and completion. The share of people who watch to the end (and rewatch) matters most. That's why every script has a re-hook at 12–15s and an open question that's only answered in the "solution" part.
  • Sends (shares by DM). Instagram has said sends are one of the strongest signals for reaching non-followers. Scripts 3, 4 and 7 are built to be sent ("send this to a friend who…").
  • Saves. Useful maths and step lists (scripts 2, 3, 7, 9) get saved, which tells Instagram the reel has lasting value.
  • Comments, especially early ones. Every CTA asks a one-word or easy question (TILT, YES/NO, your prop firm). Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  • The first 2 seconds. Instagram tests every reel on a small group first. If they swipe away in 2 seconds, it stops there. Every hook starts on movement plus big text, with no intro and no logo.
  • Originality. Film your own footage. No TikTok watermarks, no reposted clips.
  • Keywords. Instagram search reads captions and on-screen text, so say "trading journal", "revenge trading", "prop firm" out loud and write them in the caption.
  • Trial reels: Instagram can show a reel to non-followers first. Use it to test two hooks of the same script.

Posting plan

  • 3–4 reels a week, plus 1–2 carousels (Edge Notes) in between. Suggested order: 1, 2, 4, 6, 3, 5, 9, 7, 8, 10. Then repeat 8 ("Monday brief") every Monday as a series.
  • Time: 7–9 am or 7–9 pm in your audience's time zone, outside busy market hours.
  • Length 30–40s: long enough for value, short enough to finish. Cut anything that doesn't move the story.
  • Pinned comment: pin your own answer to the CTA question right after posting.
  • Pre-launch call to action: "follow for launch" or "DM EARLY". Don't say "free" or "link in bio" until the site is live.

What to record: filming guide

Shot types used in the scripts

FACEYou talking to the camera. Phone on a tripod at eye level, rear camera, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps, window light in front of you (not behind). Frame from chest up, eyes in the top third.
DESKB-roll of your setup: wide shot of the monitors, hands on keyboard and mouse, close-up of eyes, clicking Buy, standing up and walking away, coffee, notebook, sticky note on the monitor, clock, night lamp. Film each one 5–10 seconds, slowly, a few times.
SCREENReal screen recording of the app, not the monitor filmed with a phone. Windows: Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS. Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5. Browser zoom 125–150% so text is readable on a phone. Close other tabs and hide your email and real balances.
TEXT CARDBlack or brand-light screen with one bold line, 1–2 seconds, with a short sound ("boom", "whoosh", record scratch). This is the re-hook that resets attention.

Film these once and reuse them in every reel

  • Tilt alert popping up (Home Hub), several takes.
  • Quick Log done in 4 taps on desktop, plus the price wheel scrolled with your finger on a phone.
  • Edge Matrix scroll: best window, anti-window, last-month audit, blow-up radar.
  • Dashboard charts drawing in, calendar month view, the daily flex card, the public verified P&L page.
  • Prop account: Add account → Prop firm → preset → progress bars.
  • Risk calculator: typing a stop and the lot size appearing.
  • Weekly brief popup (Monday morning).
  • A wide "hero" shot of your desk at night: your signature opening and ending shot.

Audio and editing checklist

  • Use a clip-on mic if possible; otherwise record the voiceover in a quiet, soft room with the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth. Don't read; say each line, then look away.
  • Change the shot every 2–3 seconds. Cut on the beat of quiet background music.
  • Word-by-word captions in the middle of the screen (CapCut → Auto captions), with the on-screen text from the scripts on top.
  • Sound effects on the hook, the re-hook and the solution moment. Silence for half a second before the re-hook works too.
  • Export 1080×1920, 30 fps, loudness around −14 LUFS. Finish on a frame that loops back to the first shot.

Honesty rules (protect the "verified" brand)

  • Lines in [brackets] must be your real numbers or story. If something didn't happen to you, change the line or skip the reel.
  • Screenshots from the demo journal must show "sample data" on screen.
  • The app warns before a revenge trade; it doesn't block trades at the broker. Never promise profits.
