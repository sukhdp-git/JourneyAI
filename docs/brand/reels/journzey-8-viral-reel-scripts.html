Why the last reels lost people at second 3

  • The hook started too late. Frame 1 must already be moving and already have text on it. No logo, no "hey guys", no slow zoom.
  • The hook explained instead of opening a question. The first line should make them think "wait, what?", not "okay, I get it".
  • The first cut came after 2+ seconds. Cut, or change something on screen, every 0.8–1.5 seconds.
  • The payoff was guessable. If they can predict the ending, they leave. Hold the answer until the last 2 seconds.

Hook rules (the first second)

  • On-screen text is 6 words or fewer, big, in the centre of the top third.
  • Something moves in frame 1: a thumb, a number or a hard cut.
  • Voice and text say the same thing. Many people watch muted.
  • Use one hook type: contradiction, direct accusation, a question they can answer, a forbidden instruction, or a POV.
